      How it works
      
        
        
      • A new crossword drops every Saturday at midnight Toronto time (Eastern), at the same moment for everyone, wherever you are.
        • 
        
      • Twenty clues: fifteen about The Hip (songs, records, release dates, rooms they played, tours, the band, the people around them, and the podcasts) and five linking words from the music itself (instruments, the studio, the road).
        • 
        
      • Tap a square and type. Tap it again to switch between across and down. The arrows by the clue move to the next clue. On a keyboard, Enter does too.
        • 
        
      • The numbers after a clue are its word lengths: (5, 3) is two words, five letters then three.
        • 
        
      • Points: start at 100. Check word or Check grid costs 5 each time, Show letter costs 10. Finish and you keep at least 10. Give up and it's 0 for the week.
        • 
        
      • The clock only runs while the page is open in front of you.
        • 
        
      • Yer progress and stats are saved on this device only.
