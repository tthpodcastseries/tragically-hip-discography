      How it works
      
    
        
  • A new puzzle drops every Sunday at midnight Toronto time (Eastern), at the same moment for everyone, wherever you are.
    • 
        
  • Sixteen songs by The Hip hide four groups of four. A group might be an album, something in the titles, something in the lyrics, or something from the live shows.
    • 
        
  • Pick four songs and press Submit. Get a group right and it locks in. Some songs look like they fit more than one group, but each puzzle has only one answer.
    • 
        
  • You can make four mistakes. Three right out of four? It tells you you're one away.
    • 
        
  • The groups get harder in colour order: light grey, gold, red, then purple.
    • 
        
  • Points: 100 with no mistakes, then 80, 60 and 40. Use all four mistakes and it's 0 for the week. Yer points add up over every game you play.
    • 
        
  • Live counts come from the setlists in the Handbook, which are thin in the early years.
    • 
        
  • Yer stats are saved on this device only.
