The Tragically Hip Album Countdown

← Radio Show

A fan-voted countdown of all fifteen releases by The Tragically Hip: the thirteen studio albums, the 1987 self-titled EP, and "Saskadelphia". The community rates every record out of 10, and the averages set the order. jD moderates a rotating panel of three to five.

Argumentative, warm, and fan-first: the community is the authority and jD is holding the abacus. Live on YouTube Mondays at 8pm ET, then on the podcast feed every Wednesday.
