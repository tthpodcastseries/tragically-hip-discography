Getting Hip to The Hip

← Radio Show

A first-timers series into The Hip's catalogue. Each episode, Pete Marchica and Tim Lyden hear an album for the first time while jD fills in the backstory, the history, the lyrics, and the context. The fun is watching people fall in love.

The one rule: never spoil the moment of discovery. The reaction is the show. Made in support of The Downie Wenjack Fund. The run is complete, and every episode is still up and still free.
