Discovering Downie

← Radio Show

A ten-part series on Gord Downie's solo discography, from "Coke Machine Glow" to "Introduce Yerself". jD, Craig Rogers, Justin St. Louis, and Kirk Lane take one solo record an episode, always connected back to Gord's larger legacy, including "Secret Path" and Chanie Wenjack's story, told with the care it carries.

Reflective, reverent, and emotionally rich: grief and celebration at the same time. The run closed with a fundraiser for The Gord Downie Fund for Brain Cancer Research. The run is complete, and every episode is still up and still free.
