The Tragically Hip Top Forty Countdown

← Radio Show

A fan-sourced, abacus-tabulated countdown of The Hip's top forty songs. Every week jD counts down from Forty to One with a fan guest from somewhere in the world, each episode built around their Hipstory and the song that carries it. Part love letter, part therapy session, part fandom archive.

Picked up by SiriusXM The Tragically Hip Radio, with The ALS Society of Canada as the countdown's fundraising partner. The Road to the Top 40, the Flashbacks, the Round Tables, and the specials are all here too. The run is complete, and every episode is still up and still free.
