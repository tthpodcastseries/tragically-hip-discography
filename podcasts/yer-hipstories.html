Yer Hipstories: Reflections From The Final Tour

← Radio Show

An oral history of The Tragically Hip's final Man Machine Poem Tour, told by the people who were there, ten years on. City by city and night by night, jD gathers voices from across the community (concertgoers, crew, journalists, musicians, and diehards), right through to the August 20th finale in Kingston.

Raw, reverent, and deeply human. Equal parts documentary and elegy, balancing heartbreak with humour and gratitude. Every memory is told the way the guest lived it. No one talks over it.
