The Tragically Hip Interview Series

← Radio Show

Conversations with the people who made the records and ran the band, gathered from across the network's shows into one series: Jake Gold, Paul Langlois, Gord Sinclair, Johnny Fay, and Robby Baker with Craig Northey. Road stories, the making of the records, and life with The Hip. Reverent, careful, and a little awestruck: the crown jewels of the archive.

Alongside them are the conversations recorded across the network: the Introduce Yerself conversations from Fully & Completely (Stephen Brunt, Michael Barclay, Alan Cross, Dave Bidini, and more), Robby Baker and Craig Northey on Strippers Union, the Getting Hip to The Hip sit-downs, the Introduce Yerself bonus conversations from Discovering Downie, and Matt Merritt's 2013 conversation with Gord Downie.
