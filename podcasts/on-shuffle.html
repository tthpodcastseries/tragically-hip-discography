The Tragically Hip On Shuffle - Live Stream

← Radio Show

A weekly live stream through The Tragically Hip's catalogue: one song pulled at random and taken apart in real time. jD hosts a rotating panel of three (fans, musicians, writers, and fellow obsessives), and viewers join live through chat, comments, and reactions.

Conversational, curious, funny, and unscripted. Equal parts close listening and bar-room hang. Deep cuts, forgotten tracks, and the big ones all get the same hour.
