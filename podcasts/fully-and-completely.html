Fully & Completely

← Radio Show

A chronological run through The Tragically Hip's discography, from the first record to the last. jD and Greg LeGros take it track by track and album by album, with the cultural and historical context around each one. Reverent, irreverent, funny, and nostalgic, it's the show the whole network is built on, made for diehards and newcomers both.

Two runs, one show. The original (2018-2022) covers every studio record in order, with the story around each one. Fully & Completely: redux (2026) runs the whole studio catalogue again. Same DNA. Not a sequel - a reunion. Every episode is still up and still free.
