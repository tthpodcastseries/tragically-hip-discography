      How it works
      
          
        
        • Everyone gets the same Hip song each day, locked behind five scrambled words from its lyrics.
          • 
        
        • The words run from the vaguest at the top to the biggest giveaway at the bottom, and they unlock in that order.
          • 
        
        • Name the song at any time. Get it with no words unlocked for 100 points.
          • 
        
        • Each word you unscramble costs 10 points, a word you reveal costs 20, and each wrong guess costs 10. Wrong unscrambles are free - keep trying.
          • 
        
        • Hit 0 and the trunk stays locked for the day. Yer points add up over every game you play.
          • 
        
        • Squares: 🟩 unscrambled, 🟨 revealed, ⬛ never needed, 🟥 a wrong guess.
