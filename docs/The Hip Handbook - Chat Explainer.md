## 1. What it is (in plain words)

The Hip Handbook is a **free fan archive for The Tragically Hip**. Every official release, 1,358 live shows, lyrics, setlists, and hundreds of rare recordings, all in one place. No account is needed. No email, no password, nothing to buy.

- **Address:** thehiphandbook.tthpods.com
- **Made by:** jD, with data and recordings from fan communities (credits in section 7)
- **It is a fan project.** It is not official and is not affiliated with the band or their management. Never imply it is.
- **Works on phones.** It can be added to a home screen like an app.

**One-line pitch:** "A free fan archive for The Tragically Hip - every release, 1,358 shows, 625 you can play, lyrics, setlists, and more."

**Two-line pitch:** "The Hip Handbook is a free fan archive for The Tragically Hip. Dig through every release, search 1,358 shows, play 625 full recordings, and find a song by a line you remember."

**Careful:**
- "Every gig from 1985 to 2016" is the site's own wording in places, but the data includes two later shows (June 6, 2021 at Massey Hall and September 16, 2022 at the National Arts Centre). Safer to say **"1,358 shows"** and skip the year range.
- Song counts depend on setlist.fm, which is community data and can be incomplete. A "first played" or "never played" claim is "per setlist.fm", not a proven fact.
- Two pages can show slightly different counts for the same song because medleys are counted differently. Example: 'New Orleans Is Sinking' shows **831** on the Live Show Stats page and **881** on its song page (the song page counts it inside medleys too). For "most played" claims, use the **Live Show Stats** numbers in section 5 and say "per setlist.fm".
- "Never played live" is **10** on the Live Show Stats page (catalogue songs) but **23** in the Passport (the bigger official song list). Do not mix them.

## 3. What you can do on the site

Each feature has a plain name and a Hip-song name (the Hip-song name is a small gold label on the site). Link addresses are all on thehiphandbook.tthpods.com.

**The front door** (`/`)
- **On This Day:** the Hip shows played on today's date, in past years.
- **Show of the Day:** one recorded show a day, the same for everyone, with a **Play this show** button.
- **Shuffle:** Random Hip Song and Random Live Show buttons.
- **Search:** one box searches albums, songs, lyrics, and live shows. Picking a song opens that song's page.

**The Discography** - *Music @ Work* (`/discography`)
All 35 releases with tracklists, album art, and streaming links.

**Fan-Ranked Top 40** - *TTHTop40 Countdown* (`/top-40`)
The songs fans voted best, with every ranking from #1 to #169. Each ranked song links to its podcast episode (the TTHTop40 Countdown for #1-40, The Road to the Top 40 for #41-169).

**Lyrics Search** - *Poets* (`/lyrics`)
Find a song by a line you remember. (Do not quote lyrics in posts.)

**Setlist Explorer** - *Family Band* (`/setlists`)
Search shows by song, venue, city, year, or tour. Every show has an **I Was There** button.

**Interactive Tour Map** - *About This Map* (`/tour-map`)
Every gig on one map. Filter by tour or country, drag a year slider, or watch the tour play through the years. 625 shows have a full recording that plays right there in the browser.

**Live Show Stats** - *38 Years Old* (`/live-stats`)
Most-played songs, openers and closers, songs played only once, the longest gaps between plays, top covers, and songs never played live. All from setlist.fm data.

**Unplucked Gems** (`/unplucked-gems`)
A tracker of unreleased recordings, demos, outtakes and alternate versions, sorted by era.

**Video For New Recruits** (`/video-for-new-recruits`)
380 fan-shot shows, soundboards and broadcasts on YouTube, sorted by era.

**They Shot A Movie Once** (`/music-videos`)
All 36 official music videos.

**Yer Hip Links** (`/links`)
Fan sites, podcasts and the community.

**Song pages** (`/song/<song-name>`, for example `/song/grace-too`)
One page for each of 191 songs: first and latest live performance, plays by year (a tappable chart), which albums it is on, its fan ranking and podcast episode, every recording you can play, and every show it was played at. `/song` lists them all.

**Yer Hip Passport** - *In View* (`/my-shows`)
Mark the shows you were at (the **I Was There** button) and the Passport builds your concert history: shows, songs you heard, cities, rarities you caught (songs played live under 10 times ever), your most-heard songs, a map of your venues, a checklist of the 168 live songs, and a **shareable card** in three sizes (Facebook, Instagram post, Instagram story). Guests can use it, but shows are saved on that one device. A free membersHIP number saves them across devices.

**Bring It All Back** - the Daily Setlist Game (`/bring-it-all-back`) - **opens October 30, 2026**
See below.

## 4. The daily game: Bring It All Back

- **Opens October 30, 2026.** Before that, the page says when it opens.
- Every day there is **one real Hip setlist, the same for everyone**. Guess the **month and year** the show was played, in **five tries**. You need both right to win.
- Every wrong guess tells you **Earlier** or **Later** for the year and for the month, and shows a coloured square: green is the right month and year, yellow is the right year but the wrong month, orange is within 2 years, red is farther.
- Each miss also unlocks a hint, in this order: **the country, the tour, then the city**.
- After you win or run out of guesses, it reveals the show (date, venue, city, tour) with a link to **play the recording** (when one exists) and **see it on the map**.
- It tracks your **streak**, win percentage, and how many guesses you usually need. This is saved on your own device only.
- **Share your score** without spoilers. The share text looks like this:

```
🍁 Bring It All Back #1 3/5
🟧🟧🟩
thehiphandbook.tthpods.com/bring-it-all-back
```

- Puzzle #1 is October 30, then a new one every day.
- Pool: 971 real shows from 1985 to 2016.

## 6. Voice and rules for posts

**Voice:** warm, direct, conversational, a little swear-adjacent, never corporate. Sounds like jD talking to a fellow fan. Use "yer" where it fits (for example "yer concert history", "find yer shows").

**Opener for community-facing posts:** "Hey, it's jD here."

**Formatting**
- Use **single hyphens ( - )**, never em dashes.
- **'Song' titles** in single quotes, **"Album" titles** in double quotes.
- Keep it tight. Lead with the one thing that makes someone click.

**Words to avoid:** "level up", "tribe", "exclusive content", "journey", "no filler no fluff", and anything that sounds like a marketing deck.

**Naming**
- The site is **The Hip Handbook** (never "The Hip Compendium" - that name is retired).
- The newsletter is **Yer Letter**.
- "membersHIP" is only for the **free number** you can get in the Handbook. Never use it for the newsletter or as if it were a paid tier. The site is free either way.

**Always true to say:** free, no account needed, fan-built, works on a phone, built from setlist.fm, The Hip Archive, and fan trackers.

**Never say:** official, affiliated with the band, "complete" or "every show ever" (setlist.fm is not perfect), anything about getting something exclusive or paid.

**Calls to action that fit:** "Find yer shows", "Play a random show", "Search a song by a line you remember", "See if you can get today's setlist in five", "Tell me the first show you were at."

## 8. Post angles that work (starting points, not finished posts)

1. **Launch the Handbook:** "Hey, it's jD here. I built a free home for everything Hip..." Lead with 1,358 shows and 625 you can play. Link the home page.
2. **Today's show:** Show of the Day or On This Day. "On this day in 1995 the Hip played Irving Plaza in New York" - check the site for the date you are posting.
3. **The game:** teasers before October 30, then daily results ("Got it in 3 today"). Link `/bring-it-all-back`.
4. **Find yer shows:** the Passport. "Tap I Was There on every show you've seen and the Handbook builds yer concert history." Show the share card.
5. **One song, one story:** a song page. "'Grace, Too' opened 192 shows" with the song page link.
6. **A stat:** pick one from section 5 and ask fans to guess before revealing it.
7. **Rare finds:** Unplucked Gems or the one-timers. "26 songs the Hip only played once - which one do you wish you'd heard?"
8. **Prompt a conversation:** "What was yer first Hip show?" with a link to the Setlist Explorer so people can find it.
