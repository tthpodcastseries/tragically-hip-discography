      How it works
      
        
        
      • Everyone gets the same real Hip setlist each day.
        • 
        
      • Guess the month and year the show was played, 1985 to 2016. You need both right to win, and you have five tries.
        • 
        
      • Every miss tells you whether you got the year and the month right, and unlocks a hint: the country, the tour, then the city.
        • 
        
      • Fewer guesses score more: 100 points for the first guess, then 80, 60, 40, and 20. A miss scores 0. Yer points add up over every game you play.
        • 
        
      • Squares show how close each guess was: 🟩 right month and year, 🟨 right year, wrong month, 🟧 within 2 years, 🟥 farther.
