      How it works
      
        
        
      • A new clip drops every day at midnight Toronto time (Eastern), at the same moment for everyone, wherever you are.
        • 
        
      • Everyone gets the same song by The Hip each day, played live at a real show in the Hip Archive. Some tapes are soundboards, some were taped from the crowd.
        • 
        
      • You start with 2 seconds. Each wrong guess or skip plays you more: 4, 8, 15, then 30 seconds. Play each clip as often as you like.
        • 
        
      • Name it on the first clip for 100 points, then 80, 60, 40 and 20. Miss the last one and it's 0 for the day.
        • 
        
      • Get the song and you can guess the year of the show for a bonus: +20 bang on, +10 within two years.
        • 
        
      • Squares: 🟩 got it, 🟥 missed or skipped, ⬛ never needed, 📅 the year bonus.
        • 
        
      • Yer stats are saved on this device only.
